# Reader craft across the generation pipeline

New projects created through the CLI or API default to `standard.v44`; existing pins remain unchanged. Korean projects on `standard.v44` (building on `standard.v38` and `standard.v39`) use the craft context described in ADR-0120 through ADR-0123. An opening gives readers enough context to understand a choice before escalating its consequences. A hook may create curiosity before danger; description and reflection have room when they establish the current situation. The configured opening window is not a fixed three-chapter syllabus.

## Reference observations

Two licensed English translations were read on the publisher site on 2026-09-27, for narrative structure only (not Korean sentence endings):

- [The Novel's Extra, chapter 1](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/the-novels-extra/tne-chapter-1) establishes the narrator's writing failure, the email and his uncertainty about his new identity before academy activity. The narrator's explanation itself conveys character.
- [The Second Coming of Gluttony, chapter 1](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/the-second-coming-of-gluttony/scog-chapter-1) opens violently, then locates the conflict through a wounded observer's perceptions and history. Action and orientation can coexist.

These examples support allowing multiple opening speeds. They are not a frequency study, templates to copy, or evidence about Korean suffix distributions. No source prose is embedded in generation prompts.

## Context and review

Other approved concepts and accepted chapter-one contracts are frozen per project and compared structurally. Names and decorative abilities do not establish distinctness. Required tropes remain valid; their causes, social consequences and protagonist responses should belong to this story.

Inner voice includes attention, desire, humor, vulnerability and response under pressure. Setting includes physical affordances and everyday people. Writer and rewrite calls receive the same cards. Comedy history allows intentional callbacks but asks for changed consequences. Evaluators distinguish confusing or unsupported choices from unusual but motivated ones. Sentence-ending repair preserves tense and narrative voice.

All new behavior is policy gated. Old runs retain their immutable pins; new defaults do not rewrite their manuscripts. Quality must ultimately be assessed by reading fresh chapters against these criteria, not by prompt-string tests or an asserted hit-serial score.

## Identity consistency

ADR-0124 adds explicit tradition-layer selection and revised genre and voice layers. Power level does not prescribe personality. The first live concept test exposed conflicting inherited identity guidance; these revisions remove that conflict. Earlier projects keep their composed identities.

ADR-0125 replaces the automatic higher-stakes second concept with distinct character and relationship approaches. Explicit intake requirements remain authoritative. Brief narration can orient readers, and reactions from onlookers are not the only form of payoff.

ADR-0126 makes character introductions and romantic initiative follow the current encounter and individual motives. Dialogue and reflection can vary with the scene instead of alternating every spoken line with narration.

## Causal serial architecture

ADR-0127 adds a required authored episode map and a detailed opening horizon on the new policy. Every episode has its own goal and exit; a season exit is not the objective of every episode. The first chapter receives an arrival brief separating memory, observation, hypothesis, confirmation, emotional cost and first choice. Later opening chapters receive only their own causal brief. These plans remain subordinate to accepted previous-chapter text and canon. Older blueprint schedules remain unchanged.

ADR-0128 binds arrival orientation as chapter-one hard requirements for Korean possession/regression/reincarnation projects. `opening_mode: established` explicitly selects an opening after adaptation; other genres do not inherit a transmigration premise. The chapter contract carries the required experiences into normal criticism and manuscript evaluation.
