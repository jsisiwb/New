# ADR-0128: Enforced arrival requirements

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-27
- **Deciders:** operator request; live blueprint reading
- **Relates to:** ADR-0127, ADR-0010, ADR-0041

## Context

The first authored episode map still rushed from awakening to a power display. Required JSON fields alone can be filled with content that avoids the intended discovery process. The arrival requirement needs to travel through the existing hard-constraint and required-event checks, rather than relying on optional craft advice.

## Decision

Publish standard.v44 with `planning.serial_architecture.arrival_contract`. Korean possession, reincarnation and regression intakes default to `opening_mode=arrival`; an explicit `established` opening opts out. Native-world genres do not gain a transmigration premise. An explicit `arrival` mode requests orientation even for a premise without one of those genre labels, without inventing the cause of the change.

Append three deterministic, chapter-one-scoped hard Story Spec requirements with system-default provenance (user provenance for explicit arrival mode): remembered circumstances/current orientation, hypothesis and reality testing, and emotional response followed by an informed first choice. They specify required reading experiences, not fictional facts. Reserve unique requirement IDs and bind them as required events in every generated/repaired chapter-one contract before criticism and locking. Later chapters receive none of those required events. The active-constraint input budget increases in the new policy to accommodate the explicit opening requirements without trimming hard constraints. Quality gates are unchanged. The structure judge receives the actual opening, payoff, ending and required-event descriptions on this policy, extending its previous type-label-only contract view. The existing manuscript judges and canon rules remain authoritative; there is no acceptance override.

Publish 4.17.0 planner/designer/writer prompts that give these hard requirements precedence over a concept's proposed power-display hook. Character/world cards describe the initial state; future habits and achievements stay in trajectories. New-project defaults advance, while the rejected live v43 plan and all previous pins remain immutable.

## Consequences

Test language/genre gates, explicit opt-out, provenance, unique IDs, scope, idempotent binding, and propagation to actual call boundaries. A new live test includes the user's explicit arrival preference in its intake. No five-chapter quality claim is made from an unapproved blueprint or synthetic tests.
