# ADR-0136: Grade causal openings without speed contradictions

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-27
- **Deciders:** operator request for grounded Korean serial openings
- **Relates to:** ADR-0120, ADR-0127, ADR-0131

## Context

The structure judge's arrival guidance conflicted with older scoring rules: the largest crisis had to occur in the first tenth, explanatory paragraphs were counted regardless of purpose, and payoff scores privileged spectacle and audience reaction. This could reward an opening that checks recognition boxes but dismisses their emotional consequences.

## Decision

Publish structure_judge@4.19.0 with matching tradition/kr-webnovel@5 anchors and standard@46 for new projects. Grade opening interest by a concrete situation and consequential question, exposition by its causal usefulness, and payoff by meaningful change including understanding, agency or relationship. A transmigrated protagonist's response must affect behavior; a stock dismissal followed by flawless tactics does not establish adjustment. Preserve energetic Korean serial progression and a concrete next-chapter reason without requiring the largest crisis or a final-line reversal every time.

Keep the score fields, gates, budgets and all other prompts unchanged. The tradition’s numeric lint settings remain unchanged. Earlier policies remain immutable, including the ongoing five-chapter standard@45 diagnostic sample. New projects use standard@46; the current sample cannot establish live validation of the new rubric.

## Consequences

Prompt registry tests verify policy selection and old-pin preservation; policy/default and schema checks cover loading. Model judgment remains fallible and requires manuscript reading. No scoring change guarantees literary quality or a universal Korean chapter timetable.
